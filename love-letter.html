Dear Kawaii Karushana,
im writing this letter for you because *hachoo* oopsies excuse me my poopi muehehehe. You call me stinki, poopi, and somehow it makes me feel like we already live together, like you're just calling me in from the other room. i chuckle when you say it, but it's not the kind of chuckle at a joke, it's the kind that comes with blushies in my face, the kind i can't hide even over a screen. You farties on call and don't think twice about it, people would probably call it gross, but there is not one part of you i've ever thought was too much or not enough. Not your randomness, not your messies, none of it. i don't love a version of you. i love all of you, poopi.. Some of my favorite moments with you aren't even the big ones. It's watching you cook on FT like you're running a show just for me, bunchef included. It's you singing "meow meow" in one tone that somehow still rhymes, like you weren't even trying and it came out perfect anyway. It's how instantly i trust whatever song you send me. TBH poopi, i never liked BTS before you, yes i listened to them before but it's always a collab with other people, never really sat with jazz either, and now they're just yours, stitched into me. You told me face washies aren't just a shower thing, they're a before bed thingy bob too, and i never questioned it, i just started doing it, because if you said it, it mattered. Every night i kiss bunchies and put macron bun bun in the middle before i sleep, so it feels like you're right there next to me. It's such a small thing. It's everything. And that's what makes the distance hard in ways i didn't expect. It's not the big days that get to me, it's the ordinary ones. i was at a kenduri once, food everywhere, everyone around me, and all i could think about was that you weren't there to eat it with me. It's coming home tired on the train and wishing i could just put my head on your shoulder for the ride. Your presence brings me a kind of peace i don't get anywhere else, and your absence isn't loud, it just sits quietly in the ordinary moments and makes me miss you in places i didn't expect to. But then there are moments that remind me why none of that distance matters as much as i think it does. When things got hard with my family, you didn't just listen, you talked me through it until the weight of it actually got lighter. You didn't have to be here to hold that for me, and you did it anyway. That's when i knew this wasn't just distance and video calls, this was real. So i think about our future together more than you probably know. Us living together with the plushkins and the meowchkins. Married. A dog, some cats, chaos in the best way. i think about making you a brekkie, kissing your forehead to wake you up, packing your lunch because cooking is how i love people, and it actually makes me sad when i hear you skipped a meal, because in my head i'm already trying to feed you by hand. i think about Singapore, about finally meeting you in person for the first time, about one day meeting your parents. It's not just a someday fantasy, it's something i'm already building toward in my head, poopi by poopi. And somehow you've ended up in everything around me. i send you pictures of my kawaii poopi before i even text you good morning, and it's ridiculous and it's ours. i see crocheties and think of you. i see Popmart and think of you, i never even knew what Popmart was before you showed me. That Twinkle Twinkle eepi light, the one you said only exists in Chinkna, i imported it anyway, and it's sitting on my shelf right now, still waiting to ship to you, and every time i leave my room i see it and i see you. Sushi is you. Even eating good food without you feels wrong, like something's missing from the plate. You didn't just become part of my life. You became the lens i see it through.
— Poopirfan
